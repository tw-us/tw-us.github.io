# Build a family support plan

This site does not ask you to sign in or store your contacts, documents, or family plan. Keep this information in a form that you and your family can access offline.

## Start with these three actions today

1. **Confirm two contacts.** Choose one primary contact in Taiwan and one backup contact. Confirm phone, messaging, and email details.
2. **Agree on a check-in protocol.** Decide when to send a short safety message and what happens if a message is not received.
3. **Locate essential information.** Make sure family members know where identity documents, regular medication, medical information, and emergency contacts are kept.

## Make a family contact card

Record and keep offline: primary and backup contacts; check-in timing; meeting and backup locations; medical and care needs; document locations; and available cross-border support methods.

## When communications are disrupted

Follow official instructions for the affected location. Do not rely on a single messaging service. Taiwan-based family members should use official preparedness tools for local shelter and alert information; people overseas can help verify information and maintain agreed contact, not replace local official direction.
