# Build a family support plan

<div class="privacy-note">This site does not ask you to sign in or store your contacts, documents, or family plan. This is not an online form. Keep these notes in a form that you and your family can access offline, and review them together.</div>

## Start with these three actions today

<section class="notebook-sheet" aria-label="Three family preparation prompts">
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div><strong>Confirm two contacts.</strong><br>Choose one primary contact in Taiwan and one backup contact. Confirm phone, messaging, and email details.</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div><strong>Agree on a check-in protocol.</strong><br>Decide when to send a short safety message and who contacts whom if a message is not received.</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div><strong>Locate essential information.</strong><br>Make sure family members know where identity documents, regular medication, medical information, and emergency contacts are kept.</div></div>
</section>

## Make a family contact card

<section class="notebook-sheet" aria-label="Family contact card prompts">
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Primary and backup names, phone numbers, messaging details, and email</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Check-in timing and the next step after a missed check-in</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Primary and backup meeting locations</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Medical conditions, allergies, medication, and care needs</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Locations of identity documents and other essential records</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Cross-border support methods available to the family</div></div>
</section>

## When communications are disrupted

Follow official instructions for the affected location. Do not rely on a single messaging service; practice backup methods and keep essential phone numbers on the offline contact card.

Taiwan-based family members should use official preparedness tools for local shelter and alert information. People overseas can help verify information and maintain agreed contact, not replace local official direction.
