# Make a family contact plan

<div class="privacy-note">You do not need to sign in or enter anything on this site. We do not store contacts, documents, or family plans. Write these notes down where your family can use them without internet access, and review them together.</div>

## Three things to do today

<section class="notebook-sheet" aria-label="Three family preparation prompts">
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div><strong>Choose two contacts.</strong><br>Pick one main contact in Taiwan and one backup. Confirm their phone number, messaging app, and email address.</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div><strong>Agree on how to check in.</strong><br>Decide when to send a short “I’m safe” message and who will contact whom if someone misses a check-in.</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div><strong>Know where important information is kept.</strong><br>Make sure family members know where to find identity documents, regular medication, medical information, and emergency contacts.</div></div>
</section>

## Write down the essentials

<section class="notebook-sheet" aria-label="Family contact card prompts">
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Primary and backup names, phone numbers, messaging details, and email</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Check-in timing and the next step after a missed check-in</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Primary and backup meeting locations</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Medical conditions, allergies, medication, and care needs</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Locations of identity documents and other essential records</div></div>
  <div class="notebook-prompt"><div>Ways relatives overseas may be able to provide financial help</div></div>
</section>

## If you cannot reach one another

Follow instructions from authorities in the affected area. Agree on at least two ways to reach one another, practice both, and keep important phone numbers on the offline contact card.

Family members in Taiwan should use official preparedness tools for local shelter and alert information. Relatives overseas can help check information and stay in touch, but local authorities should guide decisions on the ground.
